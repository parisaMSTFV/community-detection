# Data provenance

All interactions, node identifiers, category names, edge weights, and planted communities are
generated by `src/community_detection/synthetic.py` with NumPy seed 42.

The generator creates 1,200 fictional users and 30 fictional categories across six affinity
families. Users interact more strongly with categories in their planted family and occasionally
with categories outside it. Relative edge intensity is sampled independently into an earlier
90-day window and a later 30-day window. This produces both recurring and later-only synthetic
edges. Ground-truth labels are saved separately and never enter graph construction or resolution
selection.

No customer record, company taxonomy, database export, production graph, private query, internal
schema, or real campaign result was used. Identifiers beginning with `USR-` and `CAT-` are generated
tokens and do not refer to people, products, accounts, or production categories.
