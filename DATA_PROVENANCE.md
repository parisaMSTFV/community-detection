# Data provenance

All interactions, node identifiers, category names, edge weights, and planted communities are generated by `src/community_detection/synthetic.py` with NumPy seed 42.

The generator creates 1,200 fictional users and 30 fictional categories across six affinity families. Users interact more strongly with categories in their planted family and occasionally with categories outside it. Ground-truth labels are saved separately and are not present in the interaction frame passed to Louvain detection.

No customer records, company taxonomy, database export, production graph, private query, internal schema, or real campaign result was used.
